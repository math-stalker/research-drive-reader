research-drive-reader

A personal tool that reads research PDFs from one Google Drive folder and copies them to the developer's own computer, using read-only access.

It is built for a single user, its developer. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone else.

  • Access is read-only. The tool cannot change or delete anything in Google Drive.
  • Files are downloaded only to the developer's own computer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or published.

See the privacy policy for details.
